# What Players Value: Comparing Recommended and Non-Recommended MMORPG Steam Reviews
(Kenzo Miller, Emily Ngo, Rhea Castelino, Aaliyah Bajaj, Jordan Van Remortel)

The ultimate question we have for this project is: what makes a person recommend a game or not, and what themes are most common within them?

The datasets we have include information about Warframe and Destiny 2.
